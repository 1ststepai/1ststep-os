# Evals & Quality Routing

## Principle

Cost optimization without quality measurement is unsafe.

Maintain representative eval sets for:
- interview classification
- project profiling
- module selection
- architecture recommendations
- competitor synthesis
- pricing recommendations
- Project OS generation
- code/repository recovery
- research grounding
- external-action classification

Use eval results to determine which model tier is acceptable for each task.

Do not down-route solely because a model is cheaper.
