# Full Funnel

Research → Position → Brand → Prelaunch → Waitlist → Launch → Discovery → Traffic → Signup → Activation → Paid → Retention → Referral → Expansion.

For each stage define:
- audience
- message
- channel
- asset
- CTA
- event/metric
- automation
- experiment
- owner
- next best action

Growth agents optimize business outcomes, not content volume.
