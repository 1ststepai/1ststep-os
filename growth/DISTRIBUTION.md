# Distribution & Launch Registry

Maintain a live, research-backed registry of:
- launch platforms
- directories
- marketplaces
- review sites
- newsletters
- communities
- niche vertical channels
- partnership opportunities

Each channel record includes:
- name
- category
- URL
- eligible product types
- submission mode
- cost
- account requirements
- relaunch/repeat rules
- policy notes
- fit score
- SEO value hypothesis
- expected effort
- spam/policy risk
- last verified date

The system prioritizes fit and conversion potential, not maximum submission count.
